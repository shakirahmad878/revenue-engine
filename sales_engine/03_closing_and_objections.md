# 🤝 10-Day $1,000 Revenue Engine: 15-Minute Closing & Objection Playbook

## 1. The 15-Minute "One-Call Close" Framework

Our goal on every booked call is to qualify, demonstrate immediate value, handle objections, and collect the **$500 setup payment** on the call.

```
[00:00 - 03:00] Rapport & Pain Discovery (Establish cost of missed calls)
[03:00 - 07:00] Live Interactive Demo (Trigger test SMS to client's phone)
[07:00 - 10:00] ROI & Pricing Presentation ($500 setup vs. $5,000 lost revenue)
[10:00 - 15:00] Objection Handling & Stripe Payment Collection
```

---

## 2. Discovery Questions (Uncovering the Revenue Leak)

1. *"How many incoming calls does your team get on an average weekday vs. weekends?"*
2. *"When a tech is on a roof or under a sink and can't answer, what currently happens to that caller?"*
3. *"If a homeowner calls at 6:30 PM with an urgent issue, how long before someone calls them back?"*
4. *"What is your average ticket size on a repair or new install job?"* *(Usually $800 - $6,000)*
5. *"So if just ONE missed call per week hangs up and calls the next contractor on Google, that’s costing you roughly $3,000 to $10,000 every single month?"*

---

## 3. The "Aha!" Live Interactive Demo

1. **Step 1:** Ask for the owner's mobile number:
   > *"Let’s do a real-time test right now. What’s your mobile number?"*
2. **Step 2:** Trigger a test call/webhook to simulate a missed call.
3. **Step 3:** Within 4 seconds, the owner receives the text on their phone:
   > *"Hey John, this is ABC Heating & Air. Sorry I missed your call—I'm currently on a job. Is this an emergency repair or a routine maintenance estimate?"*
4. **Step 4:** Ask them to reply with *"My AC is blowing warm air."*
5. **Step 5:** Watch the system reply back with:
   > *"Got it! We have an emergency technician available in {{city}} today between 2-4 PM or 4-6 PM. Which time slot works best for you?"*
6. **Closing Question:**
   > *"If a customer got this response in 5 seconds instead of hearing your voicemail beep, would they keep calling other contractors or wait for you?"*

## 4. Objection Handling Matrix

### Objection 1: "We already have an answering service / receptionist."
* **Response:** 
  > *"Answering services are great during business hours, but industry data shows 43% of homeowners hang up when they hear an outsourced call center or get put on hold for 2 minutes. Our system sends an immediate personal SMS in 5 seconds, locking in the lead before they dial your competitor. Plus, this works 24/7/365 with zero hold times."*

### Objection 2: "Can you send me a proposal / let me think about it?"
* **Response:** 
  > *"I can definitely send over the summary sheet. But because this is a 24-hour setup with a 100% money-back guarantee, there's zero risk. If you don't book at least 3 jobs from this in your first 14 days, you get every dollar back. Let's get your phone line configured today so you don't lose any weekend emergency calls."*

### Objection 3: "$500 is too much right now."
* **Response:** 
  > *"I completely understand watching expenses. But let's look at the math: your average repair ticket is $1,200. Catching just ONE missed call pays for the entire setup twice over. How many calls do you estimate you might miss over the next 30 days?"*
