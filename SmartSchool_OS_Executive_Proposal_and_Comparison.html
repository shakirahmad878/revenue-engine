03 The 3 Fatal Flaws of Generic ERPs at Indian School Gates

1. The Smartphone Dilemma
CBSE and State Education regulations strictly ban student smartphones on campus. Any attendance software requiring an app cannot operate for student check-in. Our durable PVC QR badges solve this completely.
2. The App Download Barrier
Less than 35% of Indian parents actively keep school apps installed due to low phone RAM, expired data packs, or lost passwords. Our 14-paise cellular SMS guarantees 100% parent reach.
3. The 08:00 AM Gate Rush
1,500 students arrive within a narrow 30-minute window (07:45–08:15 AM). Biometric fingerprint scanners are too slow and unhygienic. Our 1.2s laser scanner effortlessly clears 100+ scans per minute.
